About Me

Professional singer and piano instructor with degrees from the University of Iowa and Cincinnati's College-Conservatory of Music. I have over 20 years of teaching experience and have worked with hundreds of students of all ages. I love helping students build their skills, confidence, and passion for music.

Schaunard in La Boheme — Michael Krzankowski

I have sung internationally with the Lucca Opera Theatre, the Europaische Akademie of Montepulciano, and was a featured soloist at Carnegie Hall with the New York Choral Society.

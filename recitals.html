Recitals

Twice a year, students take the stage in front of family and friends.

Recitals are one of the most important parts of learning piano. Twice a year, every student — from beginners to advanced players — gets the chance to perform a piece they've been working on in front of a live audience of family and friends.

It's not about perfection. It's about the experience: setting a goal, preparing for it, and following through. Students learn what it feels like to be brave in front of a crowd, and they get to hear their classmates play too. The confidence that comes from a recital carries over into school, sports, and everything else.

Parents and families are always welcome. It's a celebration of the work these students put in, and they deserve every bit of applause they get.
